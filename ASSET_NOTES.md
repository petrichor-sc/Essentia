# Autumn atlas artwork

Final project asset: **assets/autumn-atlas.png**.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, then copied into this project. It is an artistic landscape, not a scientific botanical illustration, an origin map, or a rendering of the perfume’s measured chemistry. The app’s eleven markers are coded separately and remain interactive and accessible.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape art for Essentia's interactive Autumn Scent Atlas website. Create a refined, luxurious, dimensional miniature landscape photographed like a museum diorama, a little floating terraced oval island on a warm ivory paper-colored studio backdrop. An elegantly winding footpath connects three spacious botanical regions: amber deciduous trees and evergreen firs at upper left, lavender and pale white flowering shrubs in the center and upper right, a small warm cedar-and-cream woodland with autumn leaves at the bottom. At a few edges are subtly sculptural citrus and spice botanical details. These are poetic regions, not a geographic map. Warm afternoon light, richly textured miniature leaves, tactile wood, soft moss, amber, sage, muted violet, burnt sienna, pale cream. Camera elevated three-quarter overhead so it feels very 3D but every part of the island is visible. Wide 3:2 composition with generous clean ivory margins around all edges. Sophisticated editorial art direction, believable materials, shallow delicate shadows but sharp landscape detail, subtle atmospheric haze. No text, letters, logos, pins, labels, arrows, people, buildings, UI panels, bottles or giant objects. The art must be a single complete organic island, not a collage, not flat vector art, not a game screenshot.

Keep every other part of this image exactly as it is: the camera, dimensions, island shape, trees, lavender, flowers, path, lighting, textures, ivory background.
