# Botanical reference snapshot

Reviewed 5 October 2026 against Sunday Natural product and category pages and the existing Kew references. The palette contains 193 materials; 177 grouped catalogue materials have 216 documented production variants across 30 countries. Category listings and 225 product pages were reviewed. Entries group repeated sizes and distinguish species, chemotypes, plant parts and fractions.

`botanical-palette.ts` contains the public editorial data and per-variant source URLs. Production locations are not native-range assertions. Existing verified native ranges retain their references. Product labels and factual metadata are retained in German or English; olfactory summaries use the source's scent descriptors. Health and treatment claims are excluded.

The source's Ylang I category and product metadata disagree on country; both labels are preserved in `originNote`. Flouve is a 20% absolute dilution in triethyl citrate. Juniper leaf and berry oils and the frankincense/myrrh co-distillate are distinguished.

This is a dated reference snapshot. Follow the source link for current batch details and availability. Perfume-note filters preserve the classifications already present in the original palette; the newly researched materials are available through All materials and search.
